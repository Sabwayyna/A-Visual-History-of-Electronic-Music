1963
Doctor Who Theme
Delia Derbyshire

Derbyshire realised Ron Grainer's theme by cutting hundreds of pieces of magnetic tape by hand at the BBC Radiophonic Workshop. Each note was a physical splice. The alien quality comes from this material precision, sound built object by object.

Presented here as footage rather than abstraction. The Radiophonic Workshop's methods were visual: tape, splice marks, physical frequencies. The original recording is itself the document.
