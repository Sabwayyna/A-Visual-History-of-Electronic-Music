1948
Cinq Etudes De Bruits
Pierre Schaeffer

The first music made entirely from recorded sound. Schaeffer captured trains, spinning tops, canal boats, and iron lids, then cut and manipulated them in the studio at Radiodiffusion Française. He called it musique concrète - the sound preceded the score.

A single grey rectangle. Amplitude determines luminosity. The directness mirrors Schaeffer’s method: no abstraction, no mediation - only the material of sound itself.
