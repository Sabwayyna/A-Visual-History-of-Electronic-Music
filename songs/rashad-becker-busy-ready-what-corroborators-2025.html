Busy Ready What, Corroborators
Rashad Becker

Becker's work explores synthesized language, sounds that suggest speech and articulation without forming intelligible words. The title is itself a fragment: processed, unstable, teetering between syntax and noise.

Forms that gesture toward meaning without completing it. Frequency data generates shapes that accumulate and dissolve, a visual syntax that reads but does not resolve.

Each phrase carries the weight of its own ending. Compressed into fragments that teeter at the threshold of intelligibility, present then receding, never fully arriving at resolution.

The studio as a site of transformation. Raw frequencies shaped into something that resists easy categorization. Becker works at the intersection of composition and sound design, where neither label fully applies.

Timbre as the primary carrier of meaning. Not melody, not rhythm, but the grain and texture of each individual sound event. The piece rewards close listening and resists casual consumption.

A record that asks the listener to recalibrate what listening means. To sit with sounds that do not resolve, that tease intelligibility without committing to it. That's where the work lives.
